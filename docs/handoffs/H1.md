# H1 handoff: Meta tutor turn with pause and resume

Status: done, 2026-11-01. Branch `feature/meta-tutor-turn`, cut from `main` at `15bdc8b` (the merge of
`feature/board-context`). No push, no PR.

## What a learner can do now

Type a question in the composer (studio: bottom centre; mobile: above the bottom bar). Y saves the
board, writes headings, sentences and equations beside the work, and usually ends with a predict or
check card drawn on the board. Answer controls (options or free text, a confidence slider,
"Just tell me") sit under the card. The answer starts a feedback turn. Stop ends a turn and keeps
what was drawn. A refresh restores the board and the waiting card. Asking something else while a
card waits is allowed; it closes that card.

## Design as built

- **Adapter** `api/learning/tutor_provider.py`: `muse-spark-1.3`, `reasoning_effort=minimal`,
  OpenAI-compatible `chat/completions`, streaming, strict `json_schema`. It reuses the app's shared
  `httpx.AsyncClient` (`app.state.board_http`). Output is capped at 3,000 tokens and 40,000
  characters, with a 20 s read timeout and 5 s connect. Errors map to safe codes
  (`provider_timeout`, `provider_auth_failed`, `provider_output_truncated`, …), and usage reports
  cached and reasoning tokens. There are no automatic retries.
- **Constrained kinds** (contract change, see below): the live schema only offers `seg`, `write`,
  `equation`, `say`, `predict`, `check` and `turn_end`. In a first smoke run with all 12 kinds
  offered, the model emitted an `attention` widget despite the prompt. Narrowing the schema stopped
  that.
- **Engine** `api/learning/tutor_turn.py`:
  - A producer task feeds H0's `MoveStreamParser` while the consumer validates in order with
    `TurnValidator` plus two slice rules: the kind must be enabled, and a question must be the last
    move before `turn_end`.
  - An invalid move gets one repair call: non-streaming, same system prefix, at most 2 per turn,
    each charged to the budget. Later moves wait in the queue so order is kept. If the repair also
    fails, the move is dropped and recorded in the turn's timing (`dropped`). Ordering and
    kind-not-enabled errors are dropped without repair.
- **Waiting state**: a turn with an accepted predict or check ends `waiting`. That includes a turn
  stopped or cut off after the question streamed.
  - `qid = "<turn_id>.<index>"`. The board card's frame id is `y-card-<qid>`, so the controls
    re-anchor after a refresh.
  - The answer key never reaches the browser. `predict.answer` and `check.key` stay server-side
    until the learner has answered.
  - Answers are graded deterministically for predict and mcq. Free text is `correct` on an exact
    match, otherwise `unverified` (the tutor judges it). "Just tell me" is `revealed`.
- **Context packet** (`build_messages`), in cache-friendly order:
  1. Constant system prefix: `api/prompts/tutor-v1.md`, with sections `pedagogy_policy`,
     `check_writer`, `feedback_policy` and `moves` for H10.
  2. `LEARNER BRIEF` (empty until H5).
  3. `SESSION MEMORY`: lesson map of the last 24 segment goals, the last 3 turns verbatim (learner
     input plus accepted moves), and older turns compacted to goals and the question asked.
     Capped at 12 KiB.
  4. `BOARD FOCUS`: the existing `build_context` → `model_context`. Model-created elements are
     filtered out because they are already in memory; human, system and unknown elements stay
     verbatim. Up to 2 initial crops go in as `image_url` parts; no separate board-reader call.
  5. `THIS TURN`: a question, or an answer with the question, key, learner answer, confidence and
     grade.
- **Storage** `api/learning/tutor_storage.py`: new table `learning_tutor_turns`, created on first
  use with `checkfirst`.
  - A turn is claimed before any paid call. Repeating a `turn_id` replays the stored moves with no
    provider call; the same id with a different body returns 409.
  - One turn streams per session (150 s lease).
  - Budget: 60 turns per owner per hour and 600 globally (repairs count).
  - The open question belongs to the latest turn that is not failed and drew something. A stopped
    turn with no moves leaves the earlier card open.
- **Routes** `api/learning/tutor_router.py`, mounted in `learning.app` and `main`:
  - `GET /learning/sessions/{sid}/tutor` returns `configured`, `model`, `open` and the last 20 turn
    summaries.
  - `POST /learning/sessions/{sid}/tutor/turns` streams SSE events `turn`, `move`, `error` and
    `done` (status, question, timing, counts, usage). A missing key returns 503
    `tutor_not_configured` before anything is claimed.
  - Client disconnect (Stop) cancels the provider stream. The turn is recorded as `stopped` (or
    `waiting`) with a shielded DB write.
  - The proxy allows only `sessions/<id>/tutor` and `…/tutor/turns`.
- **Web**:
  - `web/src/lib/tutor-turn.ts` maps moves to the existing primitives: `seg` → `title`, `write` →
    `text`, `equation` → `equation`, and predict/check → a new `card` primitive. The renderer draws
    a framed question with lettered options in the narration column.
  - `say` appears as the composer caption; H2 will voice it. `LessonPlayer` gained `onRendered`
    for timing. `BoardController.prepare(…, capture=false)` lets the turn endpoint freeze the
    source itself. `readEvents` is now the shared SSE reader (the board teach stream uses it too).
  - `useTutorSession` adds `tutor` state with `ask`, `answer`, Stop, restore and per-stage timing
    (logged as `console.debug("[tutor] timing", …)`).
  - On a blank board the first turn starts inside the viewport. Later turns use the builder's
    `response_area` to the right of existing work.
  - Status messages from the legacy health check no longer override the tutor's status once the
    tutor service has answered.

## Contract change (H0 surface)

`tutor_moves.move_schema(kinds=None)` and `response_format(kinds=None)` gained an optional kinds
filter. With no argument the output is the same, so the checked-in `schema/tutor-moves.v1.json`
is unchanged. H3 and H8 should widen `tutor_turn.ENABLED` when diagrams, widgets, images and
pointing render, and update the prompt's "not available" line.

## Open issues and notes for later slices

- **H2**: `say` is a caption only. With TTS on, narration paces the writing, so a whole turn takes
  about 15–25 s to draw even though the first move lands in about 2 s. H2 should bind `say` to its
  segment and decide what is spoken versus written.
- **Stop after streaming ends**: queued moves are skipped on the board, but the server keeps the
  full turn in session memory. If the skipped move was the question, the card is not drawn and the
  controls dock instead. A later slice could report how many moves were rendered.
- **Placement**: later turns go to the right of all existing work (`response_area`) and the view
  pans to them. The feedback is not placed directly under the answered card yet.
- **H4**: the answer turn records `{qid, concept, dim, result, answer, confidence}` in
  `learning_tutor_turns.outcome`. That is the hook for `Observation` (idempotency key
  `(session_id, qid)`). A stopped answer turn with no moves leaves the question open, so the same
  `qid` can be answered twice. Dedupe on it.
- **H5**: pass the compiled brief to `build_messages(brief, …)` and its ids to
  `TurnValidator(brief_refs)`. Re-enable `because` in `ENABLED` and in the prompt.
- **H3/H8**: widen `ENABLED`, edit the "not available" line in `tutor-v1.md`, and render the new
  kinds in `moveToPrimitive`.
- **H8**: app INFO logs (the per-turn `y.tutor` line) are not shown under uvicorn's default
  logging. Per-stage timing is stored in `learning_tutor_turns.timing` and sent in the `done`
  event.
- **Prompt cache**: hits were sporadic. The packet order is right, but Meta's cache behaviour is
  not under our control. Re-measure on the deployed network.
- **Legacy UI**: "Teach me", the chat panel and the toolbar health text still use the legacy path.
  They were left alone except for the mobile chat bubble offset.
- `web/AGENTS.md` shows an uncommitted change written by `next dev`. It is not part of this slice.
