refuse it when the gain is not real. The evaluator works end to end on live Meta models. This run's
candidate did not beat the production prompt.

## Design as built

- **Policy:** `api/prompts/tutor-v1.md` is one prompt whose headings are the roadmap's names. GEPA evolves the
  bodies of `pedagogy_policy`, `check_writer` and `feedback_policy`. `moves` and `personalization` stay
  byte-identical (tested). Each section is capped at 1.5× its seed length, and the cap is stated in the
  reflection prompt.
- **Scenarios** (`api/policy_eval/scenarios.json`): 24 in all, 8 per persona.
  - 9 are known: the fixture beliefs. The brief names 6 of them; the other 3 were resolved in the fixture's
    history.
  - 15 are hidden: authored, and absent from every brief.
  - Splits are train 9, val 6 and test 9, stratified by persona and kind.
  - The brief is the persona's real one: the fixture is built into scratch SQLite and compiled with
    `compile_brief`, with no model call.
- **Rollout:**
  - Turn 1 is the question, through the real `build_messages`, `TurnRun` and `TurnValidator`, with repairs
    through the same adapter using the candidate prompt.
  - The student (Spark Open) answers. Then come the real `grade` and `this_turn`, the feedback turn, the
    student again, and the judge.
- **Score:** judge 0.35, resolved 0.20, probe 0.15, addresses 0.10, valid 0.10, length 0.10
  (`policy_eval/score.py`).
- **GEPA:** `gepa==0.1.4` with a custom adapter. Settings: `max_metric_calls=150`, minibatch 3, Pareto
  selection, round-robin over sections, seed 0. It is recorded as the optional extra `policy-eval` in
  `api/pyproject.toml` and `uv.lock`. The Modal image does not ship it.
- **Bounds:**
  - `Budget` is a hard cap checked before every attempt, including retries. It is cumulative per run
    directory.
  - Concurrency is 4 per provider, with one retry.
  - The call log has no headers or keys.
  - Rollouts are cached in `tmp/policy_eval/<run>/`, so a rerun does not pay twice.

## Findings

- **The gate caught overfitting.** The validation gain (+0.087) did not hold out (−0.020).
- **Noise:** the same baseline on the same scenario varied by a mean of 0.054 between repeats (up to 0.185).
  A 0.05 bar needs more held-out scenarios or repeats to be decisive.
- **The candidate rewrote only `feedback_policy`.**
  - Students were rarely wrong at turn 1: 120 of 157 answers correct, 10 wrong. So the new "after a correct
    answer, ask another forced choice with fresh numbers" rule dominated.
  - That produced calculation drills that drifted off the misconception: conv parameters without varying the
    image size, and homography plane-specificity instead of parallel lines.
  - It helped on Pauli (probe 0 → 1 on both repeats).
- **Harness limits:**
  - The Spark Open student drops its belief too easily, so `resolved` is 1.0 everywhere.
  - `valid` and `length` are at full marks too.
  - In effect only judge, probe and addresses separate prompts.
- **Live tutor side finding:** the `personalization` rule can make a turn confront an unrelated brief
  misconception. In the FlashAttention example the baseline confronted the "equal-weights" misconception while
  answering a FlashAttention question. Worth a look after the demo; no change made.

## Commands (from `api`, CMD)

```bat
uv pip install --python .venv\Scripts\python.exe "gepa==0.1.4"
.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m pytest -q tests\test_policy_eval.py
.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m policy_eval.run smoke
.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m policy_eval.run optimize --max-metric-calls 150
.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m policy_eval.run gate --repeats 2
.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m policy_eval.run report
```

To start a fresh run directory, use `--run <name>`. The default `full` reuses the cache.
