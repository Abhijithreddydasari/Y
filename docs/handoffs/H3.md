# H3 handoff: board artifacts, plus the tutor model and voice picker

Status: done (P0 and P1), 2026-11-02. Branch `feature/board-artifacts`, cut from `main` at `65b44d4`.
Brief: [H3-board-artifacts](../builds/H3-board-artifacts.md). No push, no PR.

## What a learner can do now

- **Diagrams.** Ask how a process works and Y draws a Mermaid flowchart as ordinary Excalidraw
  shapes, grouped, which the learner can move and edit. A long left-to-right chain is laid out
  top-down so its labels stay readable.
- **Widgets that respond to dragging.** These all live on the board, follow pan and zoom, and
  survive a refresh:
  - `plot`: a curve with parameter sliders;
  - `attention`: softmax weights over a short sentence, with a temperature slider, a "divide by
    √d" toggle and a selectable query row;
  - `matrix`: editable entries, the determinant, and the unit square and its image;
  - `stepper`: a derivation with Back and Next.

  With the selection tool, a click on a control works straight away. With the pen, eraser or a
  shape tool, strokes go through the widget onto the board.
- **Illustrations** (rarely, and never for maths). A dashed placeholder appears at once; the
  Muse Image picture replaces it 10–25 s later while the lesson continues. If the image fails,
  the caption stays. If the page is closed first, the picture appears on the next visit.
- **Model and voice picker** in the composer ("Spark 1.3 · Heart"; an icon on mobile).
  - Tutor options: Muse Spark 1.3 (default), 1.3 "think more", 1.2, and Spark Open on Baseten.
  - Voice options: Kokoro Heart or Michael, the browser voice, or off.
  - Unavailable options are greyed out with the reason. The choice survives a refresh.
  - A new tutor takes over from the next turn, and a new voice from the next sentence. Each turn
    records which model answered.

## Design as built

### Widget host: embeddables won (no overlay)

- A widget is an Excalidraw `embeddable` with the link `y-widget://<id>`, and its validated spec
  in `customData.yWidget`.
  - `validateEmbeddable` accepts only that scheme; any other link falls through to Excalidraw's
    own allowlist.
  - `renderEmbeddable` returns the React widget.
  - Skeletons cannot express embeddables, so the renderer emits a rectangle and the player
    retypes it after conversion (`RenderResult.embeddables`).
- **Spike results** (live, in the browser):
  - Pan and zoom: the widget followed the scene (screen x 533 → 473 after a wheel pan; width
    558 → 614 at 110 % zoom).
  - Refresh: the widget restored from the saved board, with its spec intact.
  - Pen stroke started over a slider: `elementFromPoint` hit the canvas, and the stroke was saved
    as a human `freedraw` element.
- **Why not Excalidraw's own activation.** Excalidraw sets `pointer-events: none` on an
  embeddable until a click in its centre third activates it. A first click on a slider therefore
  did nothing.
- **What we do instead.** CSS lets a child opt back in under a parent with `pointer-events: none`.
  `.y-widget[data-live=true]` turns on pointer events for inputs, buttons and `[data-control]`
  only while `activeTool.type === "selection"`; with any other tool everything stays off.
  - The widget background still selects and drags the element.
  - Excalidraw's shortcuts do not fire while typing in a widget input: typing "-3" into a matrix
    cell did not switch tools.

### Diagrams

- **Server:** `diagram_error` (in `tutor_moves`) accepts only `flowchart`/`graph`,
  `sequenceDiagram` or `classDiagram`, at most 40 statement lines, and no `click`, `call`,
  `href`, `%%{init}`, HTML tags or `javascript:`. A failure goes through H1's single repair.
- **Browser:** `mermaid-board.ts` lazy-loads `@excalidraw/mermaid-to-excalidraw` 2.2.2, the version
  Excalidraw 0.18.1 depends on, now pinned as a direct dependency. It runs with
  `securityLevel: "strict"` and no HTML labels.
  - The diagram is translated into the narration column and scaled down to fit (never up, never
    below 0.45×), with labels at 14 px or more.
  - Ids and bindings get a prefix, and everything joins one group.
  - An `LR`/`RL` flowchart wider than 1.25× the column is re-parsed as `TD`. Live: 620 px wide
    with 11 px labels before the change; 212 × 445 px with 16 px labels after.
  - If conversion throws, the labels are written as one line of text ("Query → Scores → Softmax").

### Widgets compute everything locally

- **`expr.ts`** is a recursive-descent parser compiled to closures, with no `eval` or `Function`.
  - It accepts numbers, `x`, the declared parameters, `+ - * / ^ **`, unary minus, implicit
    products (`2x`, `3(x+1)`), and the functions `sin cos tan exp log ln sqrt abs tanh sigmoid`
    plus `pi`, `e`.
  - Unknown names, a depth over 40 or more than 200 characters are rejected.
  - The server checks the same names (`PlotSpec`), so a bad expression is repaired there rather
    than shown as a placeholder.
- **`attention.ts`**: each token's embedding is a seeded hash vector (d = 8) plus a sinusoidal
  position term. Small fixed seeded projections W_Q and W_K are applied, then
  `softmax(q·k / (√d·T))` row by row.
  - The widget says "toy embeddings", so it never claims to show real weights.
  - Rows sum to 1, a lower T gives lower entropy, and turning scaling off multiplies scores by √8.
- **`MatrixWidget`**: determinant by elimination with pivoting. A 2×2 also shows the unit square,
  its image and the basis vectors, with a note for area scaling, flips or collapse.
- **`StepperWidget`**: words stay React text, and only `katex.renderToString` output (with
  `trust: false`) is injected. A test checks that an `<img onerror>` in a step stays text.
- **Speed** (mocked timing tests): a 200-sample plot redraw and a 12-token attention grid each
  compute in well under 16 ms.

### Images

- **Job start.** When the turn engine accepts an `image` move, the router creates a job row
  (`learning_image_jobs`) and writes the job id into the stored move, so the browser never sends
  an image prompt.
  - The budget is charged before the paid call: 10 per owner and 60 overall per hour.
  - The `generate` call is bounded: 2 at a time (one semaphore per event loop), with a 25 s
    timeout.
  - A style suffix asks for no text, letters, numbers or equations.
  - The WebP goes through `BoardStore.put_asset`, so it is content-addressed and owner-scoped.
  - Failures are stored as safe codes (`image_timeout`, `image_budget`, `image_not_configured`,
    …), and the turn is never affected.
- **Stop and replay.** Stop does not cancel a started job; it is already paid for, and its
  placeholder may still be on the board. A replayed turn returns the same job id with no new call.
- **Browser** (`board-images.ts`): the browser polls the status route every 1.5 s for up to 45 s
  and then swaps the placeholder for an image element.
  - The image element carries `customData.yAssetHash` and `yImage {image_id, alt, source:
    "muse-image-1.0", creator: "model"}` and is appended as `model`.
  - If a turn is drawing elsewhere (the controller's area check throws), it retries on the next
    poll.
  - A deleted placeholder is never refilled.
  - `pendingImages` resumes the polling after a refresh.

### Model and voice picker

- **Allowlist.** `tutor_models.py` holds the allowlist, with base URL and key name per provider.
  - The browser sends only `model: "<option id>"` in the turn body, matching
    `^[a-z0-9.\-]+$`, up to 40 characters.
  - The server resolves the id before any claim: an unknown id gets 422 `tutor_model_unknown`,
    and an option without a configured key gets 422 `tutor_model_unavailable`.
- **Trace.** `MetaTutor.use(option)` switches the model, effort, base URL and key. The trace
  `{option, model, effort}` is stored in the turn's timing, sent in `done`, and listed by
  `GET …/tutor`.
- **Options route.** `GET /learning/tutor/options` (identity required) lists the models and voices
  with `available` and `reason`. Kokoro voices are available when the speech engine imports.
- **Web.**
  - `tutor-options.ts` keeps `{model, voice}` in `localStorage` (`y_tutor_prefs_v1`) and
    re-validates it on load; an unavailable choice falls back to the default, or to the browser
    voice.
  - "Off" is the same switch as the Narration toggle, and the two stay in sync. "Browser voice"
    sets `LessonPlayer.browserOnly`.
  - The legacy toolbar model choice (edge/openai/cloud) is untouched and still drives Teach,
    Practice and chat. The tutor path ignores it.

## Contract changes (H0 surface)

- **No schema change.** `schema/tutor-moves.v1.json` is unchanged.
- **`tutor_moves`:**
  - `TurnValidator` now runs `diagram_error` on diagrams.
  - `PlotSpec` rejects a parameter named `x` and names that are not in `PLOT_NAMES`, `x` or the
    params.
- **`tutor_turn`:** `ENABLED` adds `diagram`, `widget` and `image`, at most one of each per turn
  (dropped without repair).
- **Public moves:** a public `image` move gains `image_id`. `TurnInput` gains an optional `model`.
- **New routes:** `GET /learning/tutor/options` and `GET /learning/sessions/{sid}/images/{id}`. The
  proxy allows both.

### Live: artifact validity and latency (Muse Spark 1.3, `minimal`)

These runs drive the real turn engine (`TurnRun`, `MetaTutor`, the same validator) from a scratch
script, without HTTP. The machine is this laptop. Times are from the start of the run to the
first accepted move.

**Run A: 20 questions with the first H3 prompt.** 15 call for an artifact and 5 do not.
- **Validity:** 20 of 20 valid, with 0 moves dropped and 0 repairs.
- **Artifacts:** 11 of 15 targeted questions got one. That was 3 of 3 attention, 6 of 7
  diagram and 2 of 5 plot questions. The misses were the KV cache diagram and the sigmoid,
  parabola and exponential plots. 0 of 5 controls got one.
- **Problem:** every attention widget reused the prompt's example sentence.
- **First move:** p50 3.05 s, p95 4.41 s.

**Same-time baseline: the same 20 questions with H1's prompt and schema** (main's `tutor-v1.md`,
7 kinds).
- **First move:** p50 2.99 s, p95 4.29 s.
- **Conclusion:** the network or provider was slower today than H1's 1.89 s; H3's longer prompt
  (+520 input tokens) costs about 0.06 s.

**Run B: the 7 misses and attention questions after the prompt fix, plus 1 control.**
- 7 of 7 targeted questions got the right artifact, and the control got none.
- Attention tokens now differ, for example "cat sat because it was tired".
- First moves were 1.86–2.98 s.

**Final run with the final prompt:** 26 turns: the 20 above plus 2 image, 2 matrix and 2 stepper questions.
- **Validity:** 26 of 26 valid (23 `waiting`, 3 `done`), with 1 move dropped (a second diagram in
  one turn, removed by the one-per-turn rule) and 1 repair.
- **Artifacts:** 20 of 21 targeted questions got one, and 0 of 5 controls did.
  - The one miss: "completing the square, one step at a time" got no stepper.
  - "Learning rate and gradient descent" got a stepper instead of a plot: valid, but a plot
    would teach it better.
  - "Backpropagation as a diagram" got a diagram and a stepper.
  - Both image questions got an `image` move. In this script run the image move was produced but
    no picture was generated; actual generation was measured separately below.
- **First move:** p50 3.14 s, p95 4.25 s, max 4.60 s. That is above H1's 1.89 s, but matches the
  same-time H1 baseline above. The H1 gate (p50 ≤ 3.0 s) was not re-met today, and the measured
  cause is the network or provider, not H3.

## Open issues and notes for later slices

- **Prompt cache and latency.** Today's p50 is about 3 s for both the H1 and the H3 prompt. H9
  should re-measure on the deployed network. Spark Open looked faster; consider it there, with
  more samples.
- **Spark 1.2** breaks the move rules when artifacts are enabled. If it stays in the picker,
  consider narrowing its kinds per option. The allowlist is the place for that.
- **Widget state is not saved.** Slider positions and matrix edits reset on refresh; the spec is
  what is saved. A tutor turn does not see what the learner did with a widget. A later slice could
  record widget interactions as evidence (H4) or put them in the context packet.
- **Images.**
  - Muse Image is slower today than in planning (median 14.4 s, up to 23.6 s). The 45 s poll window plus
    resume-on-load covers this.
  - The image budget is in the shared `budgets` table, so it works across processes.
  - The speech route still has no per-owner budget (H9).
- **Mermaid.** Unsupported diagram types are rejected on the server. Mermaid itself is about
  1 MB, lazy-loaded on the first diagram, and the first conversion is slower.
- **H8.** Show the per-turn `tutor` trace in the trace drawer. It is already in `done` and in
  `GET …/tutor`.
- **Board context.** Model-created widgets and images are filtered from the board focus, as all
  model elements are.
