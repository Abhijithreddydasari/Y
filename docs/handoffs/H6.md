## What the learner sees

The map opens from **Your mind** in Studio (a glass pill under the top-left menu) and from the brain
button beside Learn in Mobile. An amber dot means a session has been sealed since the map was last
opened.

**Zoomed out: the whole brain.**
- About 3,000 seeded points form a cortex, a cerebellum and a brainstem.
- Points near a studied region glow in that region's mastery colour.
- Region cards ("Attention · 6 ideas") fly in on tap. A region is a concept id's prefix
  (`attention.*`).

**Zoomed in: neurons.**
- **Soma**: size shows evidence weight, colour shows mastery (shaky, learning, solid). A dashed
  membrane means an early read (weight below 3).
- **Five dendrites**, clockwise from the top: recall, understand, apply, analyze, transfer. Length is
  the mean; an untested dimension is a dashed stub.
- **Misconception halo**: a pulsing red ring sized by `strength` when active, a dashed ring when
  suspected, a gold ring when resolved.
- **Synapses**: `builds_on` links. Thickness follows strength. Spikes run along them, and the target
  neuron flashes when one arrives.
- **Misfiring link**: `confused_with`, drawn as a flickering red wave.

**Interaction.**
- Drag a neuron and it springs back with a wobble, tugging the neurons it is linked to.
- Drag empty space to orbit. Scroll, pinch or use the +/− buttons to zoom.
- Tapping a neuron flies to it and opens the evidence sheet:
  - dimension bars and certainty in words;
  - misconceptions;
  - "Why it connects", with the cited answers;
  - teaching notes that cite the neuron's evidence;
  - recent questions and answers.
- Esc closes the sheet, then the map. The arrow keys cycle through neurons.

**Replay and scrubber.**
- When the profile has a revision newer than the last one seen, opening the map auto-replays it.
- The camera frames the session's neurons. A gold spike traces that session's answers in time
  order while dimensions grow and halos change.
- The scrubber steps through up to 20 revisions.

**Fallbacks.**
- Empty profile: the H0 attention arc, badged **Sample**. A load failure shows Retry and never the
  sample.
- `?mind=sample` forces the labelled sample, for demos and visual checks.
- Reduced motion or no WebGL: a still SVG with the same encodings, focusable neurons and the same
  sheet. There is no replay animation, but the scrubber still works.

## Design as built

**API** (`f25dfb3`; contract change noted below):
- The Spark Open close call's strict schema gains `links: [{from, to, kind, why, cites}]`. The
  prompt asks only for links the learner's own answers show ("no link is better than a guessed
  one").
- `keep_links` drops a link if:
  - either end is not a concept from this session or the prior profile (sent as `known_concepts`);
  - it is a self-link or has an unknown kind;
  - it cites nothing, or cites an answer that is not this session's on either end.
- At most 6 links are kept, each with `why` of at most 200 characters.
- `bridges` adds a system `confused_with` link when one named belief's cited answers span two
  concepts.
- `profile.carry_links` merges links into the previous revision's links:
  - by `(source, target, kind)`, with `confused_with` stored with source < target;
  - strength adds the weight of this session's newly cited answers only;
  - the latest `why` is kept, along with at most 16 evidence ids and the first revision.
- Links never change beliefs, the ledger or the brief.
- `GET /learning/profile/revisions` is owner-scoped. It returns the latest 20 revisions in
  ascending order, and `events` (`ObservationStore.digest`) for their observations:
  - `{id, concept, dimension, outcome, modality, created_at, question, answer}`;
  - question and answer are each cut to 160 characters.
- The Next proxy allows `profile/revisions`. The close summary adds `links`, and `notes_trace` adds
  `links` and `links_dropped`.

**Web** (`216db47`, all in `web/src/components/constellation/` apart from 2-line shell hooks, the
proxy pattern and the contract mirror):

| File | Role |
|---|---|
| `map-model.ts` | Pure revisions → frames. It handles states, due by half-life, counts (α+β−2), regions, the 150 cap, links, the replay path, changed nodes, `blend` for replay, and the H0 `ConstellationGraph` (builds_on → `prerequisite`, confused_with → `related`). `fromGraph` replays the H0 mock |
| `layout.ts` | Seeded cortex, 8 region slots, d3-force-3d (220 ticks, links as springs, region anchors) clamped inside the brain. Deterministic, so scrubbing never moves neurons |
| `MindScene.tsx` | R3F with `frameloop="demand"` and `dpr` of at most 1.75, plus a custom camera rig. Frames are requested only while the camera, springs or a replay are moving, or for 6 s after input. Labels and region cards are DOM nodes positioned per frame. `?fps=1` exposes `window.__mindFps()` |
| `MindOverlay.tsx` | Dialog chrome (glass on #0B0E14), data load, replay and scrubber, sheet, keyboard, reduced-motion and WebGL check |
| `MindStatic.tsx` | Still SVG view |
| `MindButton.tsx` | Entry point. It preloads on hover, focus or touch, portals to `<body>`, and checks for the new dot with one `GET /profile` when the browser is idle |
| `sample.ts`, `palette.ts`, `seen.ts`, `d3-force-3d.d.ts` | Sample adapter, colours, the last-seen key, types shim |

## Open issues and notes

- **Live links are unproven.** Two real closes produced none. The validation path ran live and
  dropped nothing, but the model had no second concept to link. Links need sessions whose checks
  carry different concept ids, or a prompt that lets one answer link its concept to a known
  concept it reasons from. Until then, demo spikes with `?mind=sample`. `notes_trace.links` and
  `links_dropped` say what happened.
- A typed composer answer to an open MCQ card was not recorded as evidence (second live session,
  above). Worth a look in H1/H4.
- Neurons, halos and dendrites are separate meshes per neuron, not instanced: about 5 objects per
  neuron. That is fine at demo sizes. Instance them if a real profile nears 150.
- Concept labels come from the id: `transformer.ffn-role` reads "Ffn role". A label from the
  question text would read better (H7 or H8).
- Region slots: there are 8 positions; more regions repeat them, moved inward.
- The replay shows the newest revision once per browser (`localStorage` `y_mind_seen`). The Replay
  button runs it again.
- The Next.js dev "N" badge covers the zoom-out button in dev only.
- FPS was measured with synthetic drags on this laptop, not with a finger on a tablet.
