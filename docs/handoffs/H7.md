## What changed for a learner

- **Warm-up.** At the start of a session, a pill above the composer reads "Warm-up · N due from
  last time" and offers Start or Skip.
  - Start draws a "Warm-up from last time" card: the learner's own earlier check, re-asked.
  - The answer is a normal answer, so it becomes H4 evidence. Feedback is one short segment with
    no new check.
  - The pill then offers "Next · k left" or Done. It hides once the learner asks a question, and
    the composer stays usable throughout.
- **Exit ticket.** When the session has answered checks, End session first asks "One quick check
  before you go?", offering Take it or Just end.
  - Take it draws a card labelled "Exit ticket", on the session's main concept.
  - Answering it gives one or two lines of feedback, then the session seals and reloads
    automatically.
  - If the exit turn fails, the offer comes back, so Just end always works.
- **Hint ladder.** The open card gains **Hint** (a nudge), then **Show a step** (a worked step),
  then the existing **Just tell me**. Hints appear on the card and come back after a refresh.
- **Canvases.** A pill under Mind in Studio (and in More on Mobile) lists saved sessions, newest
  first, titled by their first question, with the date, turn count, and Sealed or Open.
  - Picking one reopens that board.
  - A sealed canvas can be viewed. Its composer says "This canvas is sealed. Start a new session
    to ask" and offers a New session button.

## Design as built

**Due items** (`study.due_concepts`, pure):
- Beliefs are grouped by concept, using the lowest mean and the lowest retention
  `2^(−Δt/half_life)` across its dimensions.
- A concept is due for one of three reasons:
  - an active or suspected misconception;
  - retention below 0.5 ("fading");
  - a mean below 0.6 ("shaky").
- Ranked by misconception first, then retention × mean. At most 3 are offered.
- Shaky and misconception concepts are due at once. Without that, a session 2 run minutes after
  session 1 would have nothing due under a 24 h half-life.

**What a review re-asks** (`StudyStore.latest_checks`):
- The learner's latest **missed** check on that concept from an earlier session; otherwise the
  latest check. Missed means incorrect, partial or unanswered.
- Predicts never count, so a borrowed predict concept never becomes due (H5 note). In the browser
  run, the existing profile's shaky `linalg.determinant` (from a borrowed predict) was correctly
  not offered.
- Concepts already reviewed this session are skipped.

**Review turn**: `TurnInput.kind="review"` with `concept`.
- `tutor_router._review` stores a normal turn (`waiting`, qid `<turn>.1`) with the moves
  `seg "Warm-up from last time"`, the stored check and `turn_end`, then streams them.
- There is no model call and no budget charge, a replay works, and the key stays server-side.
- The question record carries `origin: "review"`. Its answer's THIS TURN gets `origin` plus a note
  ("feedback in one short segment … without a new predict or check").

**Exit ticket**: `kind="exit"`, one model turn.
- THIS TURN is `{kind: exit_ticket, concept, instruction}`: one short segment, then one check that
  reuses that concept id.
- The concept is the one with the most answered checks this session.
- The question carries `origin: "exit"`, and its answer's THIS TURN says to end with no new check.
- The client runs the existing close after that feedback turn.

**Hints**: `POST /learning/sessions/{sid}/tutor/hints {qid, level ∈ {1, 2}}`.
- The card must be open, and levels must be taken in order (`hint_out_of_order`).
- A repeat returns the stored text with no call (`replayed`).
- One `MetaTutor.complete` call per hint:
  - the request carries its own strict `HINT_FORMAT` (`{hint}`);
  - 1,200 max tokens, the existing 12 s timeout;
  - the per-owner tutor budget is charged first.
- The prompt has the key and the level's rule: a nudge must not state the answer or eliminate all
  but one option, and a step leaves the final step to the learner.
- `parse_hint` rejects empty or overlong text, and for mcq any hint containing a key of ≥ 4
  characters verbatim (`hint_leaked`, 502, not stored).
- Stored in the new table `learning_hints`, PK (session_id, qid, level).
- At answer time, `record_evidence` passes `hint:<level>` ids. The observation's
  `Assistance.hint_ids` carries them, so the existing ×0.7 hint factor applies at close.

**Canvases**: `GET /learning/canvases` (owner, latest 30) returns
`{session_id, title, sealed, created_at, turns}`. The web stores the chosen id as the board
session and reloads (`openBoardSession`). The sealed state comes from `GET …/memory`'s `status`.

## Contract changes (H0 surface)

- `TurnInput.kind` gains `review` and `exit`. A new optional `concept` is required for `review`.
- A question record and `public_question` may carry `origin` (`review` or `exit`).
- `GET …/tutor` `open` gains `hints: [{level, text}]`.
- `ObservationStore.capture_answer` gains `hint_ids`. `verifier_json` stores them, and
  `build_observation` fills `Assistance.hint_ids`.
- `MetaTutor.complete` takes an optional `fmt`.
- New routes: `GET …/review`, `POST …/tutor/hints`, `GET /learning/canvases`. All three are added
  to the web proxy's allowlist.
- New table: `learning_hints`, created with `checkfirst` like the others.
