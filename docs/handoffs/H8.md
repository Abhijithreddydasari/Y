## What changed for a learner

- **Pointing.** When the board holds handwritten work, Y can ring the line it is talking about in
  orange, with a short label beside it ("20 minus 5 is 15"), before explaining it.
  - The ring is outline only. Learner ink is never moved, edited or covered.
- **Feedback beside the card.** After an answer, the feedback starts under the answered card when
  that space is empty. Otherwise it goes where it went before, to the right of all the work.
- **Trace.** A "Trace" pill in the composer row (both shells, beside Memory and End session) opens a
  side drawer. It shows, newest first:
  - the browser's own stages for the latest turn: board saved, first move, first drawn, voice,
    drawn, finished;
  - each tutor turn: model and effort, total and first move, tokens, rings with their line and
    snap, dropped moves and repairs. A warm-up review shows "Stored check (no model)";
  - hints: level, time and model;
  - background evidence labels: OpenAI Decisions or the verifier, the call time, queue time, ink
    crops, and failures;
  - the session close: Spark Open notes, cited notes, named beliefs and links.

  The drawer fetches only when opened, and again after a turn while it is open. Escape closes it.
- **Glass chrome.** One `.y-glass` style covers the toolbar panel, composer, pills, Mind and
  Canvases buttons, the picker menu and the Mobile bars. The question card, picker and drawer use
  the near-opaque `.y-glass-strong`.
  - The board, ink, equations and widgets stay opaque.
  - Reduced transparency, or no `backdrop-filter`, gives an opaque white surface.
- **Reduced motion.** A global rule ends every app transition and animation at once.
- **Labels and titles.**
  - Mind labels keep the learner's spelling of capitalised words ("FFN role", not "Ffn role").
  - A canvas that only ran a warm-up is titled "Warm-up: <question>", not "Untitled canvas".

## Design as built

- **`pointing.resolve`** (`api/learning/pointing.py`, pure):
  1. Map the model's 0–1000 point to board coordinates over the sent crop's bounds, which include
     the export padding.
  2. **Lines:** letters (strokes at least 0.35 × the 75th-percentile height) form lines by vertical
     overlap of at least 40%. Flat strokes (minus, equals, bars) then join the nearest line.
  3. **The reported line wins** when it is in range (`snap: "line"`). Otherwise use the line under
     the raw point (`nearest`). With no human ink, draw a 60 px ring at the raw point (`raw`).
  4. A gap of 3 line heights splits two pieces of work on one row, and the piece nearest the point
     is kept.
  5. Pad the box by 10 px, and store `at`, `raw`, `snap` and `lines` in the move.
- **Router.** The crops sent as images are remembered in order, so `point.crop` indexes the same
  list. Each point is resolved as it streams, like `image`. A replay returns the stored ring with no
  call. The turn timing gains `points: [{snap, line, lines}]`.
- **Schema per turn.** `MetaTutor` offers `point` only when the messages carry an `image_url`
  part (two prebuilt formats). The engine allows at most 2 points per turn. A point with no crop,
  or a third point, is dropped without repair.
- **Prompt.** The "Not available: point" line is replaced by a `point` rule: learner ink only,
  never the tutor's output; `line` counted from the top; a label of at most 6 words; placed at the
  start of the segment; the first wrong line when the learner asks for a check.
- **Web.**
  - `board-pointing.ts` builds the ellipse at √2 × the box, so every corner of the ink box is
    inside the ring. The label goes right of the ring, else above, else below, at the first spot
    clear of other elements.
  - Rings are tagged `customData.ySemantic.kind = "point"` and appended as `model`.
    `LessonPlayer.enqueueStep` keeps stream order and skips the step after Stop.
  - `validateOutput` exempts ring elements from the response-area check.
- **Trace route.** `GET /learning/sessions/{sid}/trace` is owner-scoped and proxied. It reads only
  stored timings:
  - turn `timing` (with provider timing and usage);
  - `learning_hints.timing` (`StudyStore.hint_timings`);
  - evidence rows (the view gains `label: {provider, model, provider_ms, queue_ms, images}`);
  - the sealed close summary.

  It returns no texts beyond each turn's first segment goal, and no keys or prompts. Turn history
  rows gain `at`.
- **Frame probe.** `?fps=1` exposes `window.__boardFrames(reset)` with p50, p95, max and the count
  over 20 ms. `?glass=0` switches the chrome to opaque for the comparison.

## Contract changes (H0 surface)

- `tutor_moves.Point` and `schema/tutor-moves.v1.json` are unchanged.
  `tutor_turn.ENABLED` adds `point`, and `MAX_POINTS = 2`.
- Stored and public `point` moves gain `at`, `raw`, `snap` and `lines` from the server. The web
  `PublicMove` mirrors them.
- **New route:** `GET …/trace`, added to the proxy allowlist.
- **Evidence route:** each row gains `label`.
- **Turn history:** a turn view gains `at`.

### Live (paid; this laptop, Muse Spark 1.3 `minimal`, `scripts/live_pointing.py`)

- **Ink.** Jittered single-stroke glyphs, one freedraw element per pen stroke, about 40 px letters.
- **Problems.** Four, each with one planted wrong line: `3x = 25`, `2x = 6`, `x = 5`, `x = 6`.
- **Crop.** Rendered white with 24 px padding at 2×, sent through the real turn route.

| Run | Turns | With a point | Reported line right | Raw point in the wrong line's box | Snapped ring on it (and no other line) | First move p50 / max |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pointing on | 12 | 11 | 11/11 | 7/11 | **11/11** | 7.06 s / 17.1 s |
| Same-time baseline, `point` removed from the schema | 8 | — | — | — | — | 6.14 s / 9.5 s |

- **Hit rate.** Snapping lifts ring hits from 7/11 (raw) to 11/11. That matches the planning
  measurement: the line is reliable and the raw point is not.
- **Labels** were short and specific: "3x = 25 should be 15", "10 minus 4 is 14", "15 divided by 3
  is 5". One turn gave no point.
- **Latency.**
  - Reading an ink image is the slow part: 6.1 s p50 without `point`, against about 2–3 s for
    text-only turns earlier today.
  - Pointing adds about 0.9 s at p50 on these small samples.
  - **This is above the H1 gate for ink turns.** See open issues.

### Browser journey (in-app browser, `next start` on 3015 → API on 8113, real providers)

The API ran against a **copy** of `api/data/learning.sqlite3` in the session scratchpad, so the dev
profile is untouched.

1. **Pointing (Studio, 1280-wide pane).**
   - Wrote `3x + 5 = 20 / 3x = 25 / x = 25/3` with synthetic pen strokes (25 freedraw elements,
     through Excalidraw's own pointer handling), then asked "Is my working right?".
   - The orange ring went around `3x = 25`, labelled "20 minus 5 is 15". "Spot the first mistake"
     was written beside the work, ending in a check.
   - Afterwards all 25 ink records were still `human/human` with 0 `mixed`. The ring and label are
     transparent `model` elements.
   - Client first move 6.59 s; the server's first move was 6.33 s, with 5,553 input tokens.
2. **Answer B (wrong).** The feedback "Fix 3x equals 15" started under the answered card.
   - Bug found and fixed: it sat flush against the card, because the card grows 46 px with "You
     answered…" after placement. The spot now starts 90 px below the card. This was covered by a
     test and not re-run in the browser.
   - The evidence label ran in the background: OpenAI Decisions, call 3.93 s, queued 12 ms.
3. **Trace drawer (Studio).** The drawer listed the browser's stages, the turn, `Muse Spark 1.3 ·
   minimal`, tokens and "1 ring (line 2, line)". Tweaks found here and fixed:
   - browser stages are now sorted by time, since voice starts after drawing ends;
   - the repeated "Browser" badge is gone;
   - the drawer uses the stronger glass so text over the green Teach button stays legible.
4. **Mobile shell (375 × 812).** The Trace pill is in the composer row, and the drawer opens full
   height with both turns and the label row.
   - In the Studio shell at phone width, the legacy chat orb covers the pill. That is the Mobile
     shell's job, so it was left alone.
   - The pills were first placed at the top left. They covered Excalidraw's style panel while the
     pen was active, so Trace moved into the composer row.
5. **Glass frame times** (pane visible, about 165 Hz display, a scripted 3 s pen stroke passing
   under the composer and pills, 3 runs each):

| Chrome | p50 | p95 | max | frames > 20 ms |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Glass on | 6.1 ms | 6.4–6.5 ms | 6.7–11.7 ms | 0 |
| `?glass=0` (opaque) | 6.1 ms | 6.4–6.5 ms | 6.5–11.7 ms | 0 |

   Glass costs nothing measurable here, so no opaque fallback was needed.

## Open issues and notes for later slices

- **Ink turns are slow** (decision needed): every turn whose focus holds learner ink re-sends up to
  2 crops.
  - Measured first moves: 6–7 s p50, 17 s max live, and 22.2 s on the browser answer turn.
  - Options:
    1. send crops only on question turns, or when the ink changed since the last turn;
    2. send one crop instead of two;
    3. route ink turns to Spark Open.

  Not changed here, because it trades accuracy (the feedback turn re-reads the work) for latency.
